# ADR-0002: Web-First Monetization via Stripe Managed Payments (MoR) & Decoupled Entitlements

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-26

Gainz AI launches web/PWA-first from Germany as a solo-operated product at 7.99 EUR/month, requiring zero fixed SaaS overhead, automated global B2C VAT/invoicing/dispute handling, a generous usage-based starter experience, tester invitations, and future extensibility to native mobile app stores. We adopt **Stripe Managed Payments** (Stripe's Merchant of Record solution via Link) using server-generated Hosted Checkout Sessions and Stripe Billing Customer Portal redirects, while decoupling payment vendor state from application access through a unified server-side **`Entitlement`** model in Supabase (`Subscription` | `InviteGrant` | `FreeAllowance`). Manual workout logging, Google Sheets synchronization, and deterministic training insights remain un-gated offline and post-cancellation; only upstream AI coaching and classification endpoints enforce active `Entitlement` on the server.

## Considered Options

- **Lemon Squeezy (MoR)**: Closed to new store sign-ups on 2026-11-16 ahead of its full shutdown on 2027-03-31 following consolidation into Stripe Managed Payments; also demonstrates the platform-sunset risk of smaller third-party MoR wrappers.
- **Paddle Billing (MoR)**: Strong independent MoR with native PayPal subscription support, rejected for initial launch due to higher per-transaction fixed fees at 7.99 EUR (~0.90 EUR vs. ~0.65 EUR), manual compliance/underwriting review friction for fitness coaching software, and lack of native Stripe-level unification when later adding RevenueCat for iOS/Android.
- **Vanilla Stripe Billing (Direct Seller, Non-MoR)**: Slightly lower processing fees, rejected because acting as direct legal B2C seller introduces multi-jurisdiction VAT/OSS filing liability, paid add-ons for tax/invoicing, per-customer B2C invoice bookkeeping, and direct chargeback exposure for a solo operator.
- **RevenueCat + Native In-App Purchases (Apple / Google)**: Deferred until native iOS/Android store distribution; choosing Stripe now ensures native compatibility with RevenueCat later while the decoupled `Entitlement` model requires zero client or `ai-coach` gate changes.

## Consequences

- **Pseudonymous Anti-Abuse Digest (24-Month TTL) & True Cost Anonymization**: Deleting an account immediately revokes `Entitlement` and erases personal rows—cancelling any active Stripe subscription synchronously or recording only the non-personal `provider_subscription_id` in a self-healing tombstone if Stripe is temporarily unreachable—while handling GDPR Art. 5(1)(e) / Art. 6(1)(f) / Art. 17(3)(e) across two distinct ledgers: `FreeAllowance` consumption is keyed by a versioned pseudonymous `v1:HMAC-SHA256(pepper, sub)` digest with a bounded 24-month inactivity retention window, whereas monthly `TokenUsage` cost rows replace the user identifier with one unlinked `'deleted:' || gen_random_uuid()` key per `(user_id, year_month)` for true, irreversible anonymization.
- **Canonical Webhook Reconciliation**: Webhook deliveries are deduplicated by Stripe `event.id`, reconcile subscription state via a canonical `GET /v1/subscriptions/{id}` lookup, and commit atomically behind a monotonic `last_event_created` + terminal-status write guard.
- **Statement Descriptor Clarity & Payment Methods**: Because Stripe Managed Payments processes sales through Stripe/Link (`LINK.COM*` statement descriptor) and focuses on Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, and cards rather than PayPal subscriptions, the Upgrade Card explicitly discloses billing via Stripe / Link to prevent unrecognized-charge disputes, and checkout sales are restricted to Managed Payments tax-covered countries.
- **Pause-Safe Starter Habit Loop & Strict Identity Binding**: `FreeAllowance` meters completed AI requests rather than calendar days (with client-triggered briefings running only when the athlete opens the app, never via background server crons) so illness or breaks never expire starter access, and Checkout Sessions bind the verified Google OIDC `sub` onto both `client_reference_id` and `subscription_data.metadata.user_id`.
