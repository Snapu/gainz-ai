# 22: GDPR Account Deletion with Subscription Tombstone & Bilingual Legal Disclosures

**Parent:** TICKET-017  
**Labels:** `ready-for-agent`, `tracer-bullet`, `application`, `infrastructure`, `presentation`  
**Blocked by:** TICKET-019, TICKET-020, TICKET-021  
**Status:** ready-for-agent

**What to build:** Complete pre-launch compliance and account lifecycle safety so deleting an account always revokes AI `Entitlement` and erases personal app data immediately under GDPR—cancelling any active Stripe subscription synchronously when Stripe responds, or recording a non-personal self-healing cancellation tombstone if Stripe is temporarily unreachable—while truly anonymizing monthly AI cost analytics, preserving the 24-month pseudonymous `FreeAllowance` anti-abuse digest, and updating bilingual (`de` / `en`) Terms of Use and Privacy Policy disclosures.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Database schema adds `pending_subscription_cancellations(provider_subscription_id primary key, created_at, last_error)` containing zero personal `user_id` or email columns, locked down with RLS to `service_role` only.
- [ ] When `delete-account` is called for a user with a Stripe subscription in any non-terminated state (`active`, `trialing`, `past_due`, `unpaid`, `paused`), the handler attempts `DELETE /v1/subscriptions/{provider_subscription_id}` synchronously (treating `404` / `resource_missing` / already `canceled` idempotently as cancelled).
- [ ] Regardless of whether Stripe succeeds immediately or suffers a transient outage, `delete-account` executes one atomic database cleanup RPC that **always immediately** deletes `user_subscriptions`, `invite_grants`, `ai_daily_usage`, and `ai_request_results` for `user_id`, replaces `actor_key` in `ai_monthly_token_usage` with **one** fresh unlinked `'deleted:' || gen_random_uuid()` per `(user_id, year_month)` (`is_anonymized = true` so monthly P50/P95 percentiles remain exact without cross-month linkage), retains only the versioned pseudonymous `free_allowance_usage` digest, and—only if upstream Stripe cancellation failed—inserts `provider_subscription_id` into `pending_subscription_cancellations`, returning `{ deleted: true, googleRevoked, billingCancellationPending }`.
- [ ] If `billing-webhook` subsequently receives any Stripe event for a `subscription_id` present in `pending_subscription_cancellations`, it immediately cancels the subscription on Stripe (`DELETE /v1/subscriptions/{id}`), removes the tombstone, and returns `200` without recreating any `user_subscriptions` row.
- [ ] `DeleteAccountSheet` informs subscribers before confirmation that deleting their account automatically cancels their active subscription, and surfaces a clear localized notice on completion if `billingCancellationPending` is true; local billing cache is purged alongside other local state on account deletion.
- [ ] Bilingual (`de` / `en`) `TermsOfUse` and `PrivacyPolicy` views are updated to accurately disclose the 7.99 EUR/month subscription, monthly period-end cancellation, `FreeAllowance` & `InviteGrant` rules, **Stripe / Link** as Merchant of Record recipient, the 24-month inactivity-bounded pseudonymous `HMAC-SHA256` anti-abuse digest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), Art. 17(3)(e), Art. 5(1)(e)), and irreversible random-UUID anonymization of aggregated AI token cost metrics.
- [ ] Behavioural seam tests cover `delete-account` with active subscription (synchronous cancel), Stripe outage during deletion (immediate personal erasure + tombstone creation + `billing-webhook` self-healing cancellation), non-subscribed user deletion, per-`(user_id, year_month)` random-UUID cost anonymization, preservation of consumed `FreeAllowance` when re-authenticating with the same Google `sub`, and `DeleteAccountSheet` / legal view rendering.
