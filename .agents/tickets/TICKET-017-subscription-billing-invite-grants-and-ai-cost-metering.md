# 17: Subscription Billing, Starter Free Allowance, Invite Grants & AI Cost Metering

**Status:** ready-for-agent  
**Labels:** `ready-for-agent`, `domain`, `application`, `infrastructure`, `presentation`

---

## Problem Statement

Gainz AI is preparing for public production launch as a solo-operated product in Germany, but currently has no monetization mechanism, no way to gate server-side AI coaching costs after a new athlete experiences the product, no invitation mechanism for beta testers to access Pro AI features without paying, and no visibility into actual upstream Gemini AI costs per athlete, per AI feature type, or on average across the user base.

Without these capabilities before launch:

1. Every authenticated user could consume server-funded Gemini tokens indefinitely without generating revenue, creating unbounded financial exposure.
2. Putting a hard paywall immediately after sign-up would block new athletes before they experience Gainz AI's core habit loop (generating a personalized `TrainingPlan`, receiving proactive daily coaching briefings, and seeing multi-week `e1RM` and `VolumeLandmarks` progression), hurting conversion—especially if illness or schedule disruptions pause their first training weeks.
3. Operating direct B2C subscription billing as a one-person business in Germany would create disproportionate B2C invoice handling, multi-country VAT/OSS, dunning, chargeback, and statutory cancellation compliance overhead unless handled by a Merchant of Record with zero fixed monthly fees.
4. Invited testers need frictionless complimentary access (even before live commercial payout verification completes) without weakening security or fair-use protections, and users who delete and recreate their account must not be able to reset complimentary starter budgets indefinitely.
5. Because upstream LLMs bill strictly by input, cached input, output, and reasoning tokens across primary and fallback models, simple request counts cannot reveal true per-athlete or per-action unit economics and margin against a 7.99 EUR/month subscription.

## Solution

Introduce a unified, provider-decoupled **`billing` Bounded Context** and server-side **`Entitlement`** architecture paired with **Stripe Managed Payments (Merchant of Record via Stripe / Link)** at **7.99 EUR/month (incl. VAT)** and **granular Gemini `TokenUsage` cost metering**:

- **Pause-Safe Starter Habit Loop (`FreeAllowance`):** Every newly authenticated athlete receives a one-time starter budget of **30 complimentary AI coaching requests** (capped at 5/day during the free phase to prevent single-day chat exhaustion) plus **40 background exercise classification calls**, with **no calendar expiration**. Because session briefings are triggered strictly on client app open (never by server cron jobs), illness or rest weeks while the app remains closed consume zero starter budget, while opening the app delivers full motivational coaching briefings.
- **Un-Gated Offline Gym Core:** Manual workout logging (`ExerciseLog`), Google Sheets offline-queued synchronization, rest timers, and deterministic `trainingInsights` (`ACWR`, `e1RM`, `VolumeLandmarks`, fatigue triggers) remain permanently free and 100% offline-capable, including after subscription cancellation. Only server-side AI endpoints require an active `Entitlement`.
- **Low-Admin Subscription Checkout & Self-Service (`Subscription`):** Athletes upgrade for **7.99 EUR/month (incl. VAT, cancellable monthly to period end)** via a server-generated Stripe Checkout Session redirect with **Managed Payments enabled** (`managed_payments.enabled = true`), bound cryptographically to their verified Google identity—requiring zero third-party client JavaScript and zero Content-Security-Policy relaxation while offloading global B2C VAT remittance, customer receipts, dunning retries, and dispute handling to Stripe/Link. Failed renewals grant a **3-day `past_due` grace period** alongside Stripe Smart Retries so athletes are never locked out mid-workout in the gym. Subscribers manage payment methods, receipts, and 1-click cancellation through a signed Stripe Billing Customer Portal redirect in the global header menu.
- **Frictionless Tester Access (`InviteGrant`):** Founders create single- or multi-use invitation codes directly in Supabase (with configurable `max_uses` and optional duration in days or unlimited access). Testers redeem codes via a shareable `?invite=CODE` link or directly on the Upgrade Card, unlocking AI coaching immediately under standard daily fair-use limits (`15` coach / `50` classify per UTC day).
- **Privacy-Sound, Abuse-Safe Account Deletion:** Deleting an account revokes `Entitlement` and erases personal app data immediately—cancelling any active Stripe subscription synchronously when Stripe is reachable, or recording only the non-personal `provider_subscription_id` in a self-healing cancellation tombstone if Stripe is temporarily down—while retaining a versioned pseudonymous `HMAC-SHA256` subject digest on `FreeAllowance` usage with a **24-month inactivity TTL** and truly anonymizing historical cost rows via an unlinked random UUID (`deleted:<uuid>`).
- **Granular Per-Action & Per-Athlete AI Cost Analytics (`TokenUsage`):** Every upstream Gemini invocation (including partial or failed fallback attempts, never reversed by user quota refunds) captures `prompt`, `cached`, `output`, and `thinking` tokens tagged by granular `request_kind` (`initial_plan`, `plan_update`, `session_briefing`, `chat`, `classify`), paired with a configurable global daily spend cap (`AI_DAILY_COST_CAP_USD`) and ready-to-query SQL views in Supabase Studio.

## Implementation Decisions

- **New `billing` Bounded Context & Strict 4-Layer Architecture:**
  - Introduce a dedicated `billing` module adhering strictly to Gainz AI's 4-layer DDD separation (`domain`, `application`, `infrastructure`, `presentation`) and cross-module facade rules (including shared-presentation cycle checks: shared header/account leaf Vue views may import the `billing` presentation facade, while `billing` presentation `.ts` modules never import shared presentation files that depend back on `billing`).
  - **`domain`:** Models the pure `Entitlement` aggregate/value object (`source: 'subscription' | 'invite_grant' | 'free_allowance' | 'none'`, `evaluatedAt` server timestamp, `{ coachUsed, coachLimit: 30, classifyUsed, classifyLimit: 40 }`), `SubscriptionStatus`, grace-period state, independent action eligibility predicates (`canUseCoach` vs. `canUseClassify`, `isInPastDueGrace`—so exhausting 40 background classifications never locks out remaining free coaching requests!), and semantic domain error unions (`'payment-required' | 'invite-invalid' | 'invite-exhausted' | 'invite-expired' | 'invite-already-redeemed' | 'billing-unavailable' | 'unauthorized'`). Zero external or Stripe-specific types leak into the domain.
  - **`application`:** Exposes typed `neverthrow` `ResultAsync` use cases (`fetchEntitlement`, `startSubscriptionCheckout`, `openCustomerPortal`, `redeemInviteCode`) against an abstract, provider-neutral `BillingGateway` port.
  - **`infrastructure`:** Implements the HTTP client adapter to the backend `billing` Edge Function guarded by Zod Anti-Corruption Layer schemas (including strict `https://*.stripe.com` URL validation before top-level checkout/portal navigation), alongside a synchronous scoped `localStorage` snapshot cache adapter (matching all other non-secret domain caches, reactive to sign-out and `purgeLocalStorage` without async cold-start races).
  - **`presentation`:** Exposes `useBillingStore` and localized presentation helpers through the module's presentation barrel facade. Top-level tab views (`CoachTab`, `PlanTab`) never call stores directly and instead consume billing state via their respective view-model composables.

- **Entitlement Precedence & Pause-Safe `FreeAllowance` Rules:**
  - Effective `Entitlement` is evaluated deterministically on the server in strict priority order:
    1. **Active `Subscription`:** Status is `active` or `trialing` (including scheduled period-end cancellations where `cancel_at_period_end = true` and `current_period_end > now()`), OR status is `past_due` within the 3-day `grace_ends_at > now()` window. Grants `15` `coach` and `50` `classify` requests per UTC day without consuming `FreeAllowance`.
    2. **Active `InviteGrant`:** Joined against its parent `invite_codes` record with `invite_codes.is_active = true`, `invite_grants.revoked_at IS NULL`, and `invite_grants.expires_at IS NULL OR invite_grants.expires_at > now()` (ensuring both per-user and instant per-code revocation take effect immediately). Grants `15` `coach` and `50` `classify` requests per UTC day without consuming `FreeAllowance`.
    3. **Remaining `FreeAllowance`:** `Entitlement.source` remains `'free_allowance'` while either `coach_used < 30` or `classify_used < 40`, while each action independently checks its respective pool (`coach_used < 30` with `5`/day cap for `coach`; `classify_used < 40` with `20`/day cap for `classify`), keyed by the pseudonymous subject digest. Proactive daily briefings occur strictly on client app open (never via server background crons) and remain enabled while `canUseCoach` is true.
    4. **Action Unentitled (`402 payment-required`):** Denies AI execution for the exhausted action with HTTP `402` (`error: "payment-required"`) and returns the full action-independent `Entitlement` payload.

- **Pseudonymous Anti-Abuse Digest (24-Month TTL, Rotation-Safe) vs. True Cost Anonymization:**
  - Require `ABUSE_PREVENTION_PEPPER` (`>= 32` characters) unconditionally in server configuration, computing a version-prefixed `subject_digest = 'v1:' || HMAC_SHA256(ABUSE_PREVENTION_PEPPER, google_sub)` (supporting optional `ABUSE_PREVENTION_PEPPER_PREVIOUS` fallback lookup during future secret rotation without resetting consumed starter budgets).
  - Treat `subject_digest` in `free_allowance_usage` as **pseudonymous personal data** processed under GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and Art. 17(3)(e) solely to prevent repeated starter-allowance redemption, and enforce a **24-month inactivity retention limit** (probabilistically pruning rows where `updated_at < now() - interval '24 months'`).
  - Keep `ai_monthly_token_usage` completely separate from `subject_digest` upon account deletion: inside one atomic account-deletion cleanup RPC, assign **one** fresh unlinked `'deleted:' || gen_random_uuid()` key per `(user_id, year_month)` (`is_anonymized = true`), achieving irreversible true anonymization without splitting a single athlete's monthly rows across multiple synthetic user keys (preserving exact monthly P50/P95 percentiles).

- **Database Schema, Lock Ordering & Atomic RPC Contracts (Supabase PostgreSQL with `service_role`-only RLS):**
  - Lock down all new tables, views (`WITH (security_invoker = true)`), and RPCs with Row-Level Security enabled, explicit `REVOKE ALL` from `public`, `anon`, and `authenticated` (overriding Supabase `public` default privileges), explicit `GRANT` only to `service_role`, and `SET search_path = ''` on all PL/pgSQL functions.
  - **`free_allowance_usage`:** `(subject_digest text primary key, coach_used integer not null default 0, classify_used integer not null default 0, updated_at timestamptz not null default now())` plus index on `(updated_at)` for 24-month pruning.
  - **`user_subscriptions`:** `(user_id text primary key, provider text not null, provider_subscription_id text unique not null, provider_customer_id text not null, price_id text not null, status text not null, cancel_at_period_end boolean not null default false, current_period_end timestamptz, grace_ends_at timestamptz, last_event_created bigint not null default 0, updated_at timestamptz not null default now())`.
  - **`processed_webhook_events`:** `(event_id text primary key, processed_at timestamptz not null default now())` plus index on `(processed_at)` for 30-day probabilistic pruning.
  - **`pending_subscription_cancellations`:** `(provider_subscription_id text primary key, created_at timestamptz not null default now(), last_error text)` containing zero personal `user_id` or email fields—used strictly as a self-healing cancellation tombstone when Stripe is unreachable during `delete-account`.
  - **`invite_codes`:** `(code text primary key, max_uses integer not null check (max_uses > 0), used_count integer not null default 0 check (used_count >= 0), grant_duration_days integer check (grant_duration_days is null or grant_duration_days > 0), expires_at timestamptz, is_active boolean not null default true, note text, created_at timestamptz not null default now())`.
  - **`invite_grants`:** `(user_id text primary key, code text not null references invite_codes(code), granted_at timestamptz not null default now(), expires_at timestamptz, revoked_at timestamptz)` plus index on `(code)`.
  - **`ai_daily_global_usage` extension & `ai_monthly_token_usage`:** Add `estimated_cost_usd_micros bigint not null default 0 check (estimated_cost_usd_micros >= 0)` directly onto `ai_daily_global_usage` (PK `usage_date`) for `O(1)` lock-free daily spend circuit-breaking! Store pre-aggregated monthly rollups in `ai_monthly_token_usage` keyed by `(actor_key text, year_month char(7), model text, request_kind text, entitlement_source text)` with `is_anonymized boolean`, `request_count`, `unmeasured_count`, `prompt_tokens`, `cached_tokens`, `output_tokens`, `thinking_tokens`, `estimated_cost_usd_micros bigint`, and `updated_at`.
  - **Deadlock-Free Lock Ordering, Per-Request Token Bounds, Spend Circuit-Breaker & Idempotent Refund RPCs:**
    - Enforce identical lock ordering across both authorization and refund RPCs (`ai_daily_usage(user_id, usage_date)` -> `free_allowance_usage(subject_digest)` -> `ai_daily_global_usage(usage_date)`) so concurrent `classify` admissions and `coach` refunds for the same athlete can never deadlock.
    - Bound single-request worst-case spend upfront via the existing 100 KB request body cap, explicit `maxOutputTokens` caps in `generationConfig` per `request_kind` (`planning` vs. `execution` vs. `classify`), and hard max 2 model attempts (`primary` + `1 fallback`) per logical request—preventing runaway per-request generation while avoiding leaked 2-phase cost reservations if an Edge isolate terminates mid-stream.
    - When `AI_DAILY_COST_CAP_USD` (checked in `O(1)` against `ai_daily_global_usage.estimated_cost_usd_micros`, soft-bounded by tightly capped in-flight streams) or the global request cap trips, return `global_capped = true` -> HTTP `429 rate-limit-exceeded` (never `402`, so paying subscribers never see an Upgrade Card during a global cap event).
    - Pass the admission-time `admitted_source` boolean (`admitted_source = 'free_allowance'`) into the refund RPC, decrement `free_allowance_usage` only `IF FOUND` on the `ai_daily_usage` decrement (`count > 0`), and gate coach failure settlement on an atomic `status = 'processing' -> 'failed'` transition so refunds are strictly idempotent per request and **never** reverse recorded API costs in `ai_monthly_token_usage` or `ai_daily_global_usage.estimated_cost_usd_micros`.

- **Edge Function Architecture & Gateway Config (`billing`, `billing-webhook`, `ai-coach`, `delete-account`):**
  - Configure both `billing` and `billing-webhook` with `verify_jwt = false` at the Supabase function gateway (since callers use Google OIDC/OAuth tokens or Stripe HMAC signatures rather than Supabase Auth JWTs), and extend Edge Function boundary checks so shared helpers never import function-specific directories.
  - **`billing` Edge Function:** Authenticates callers via the existing Google OIDC ID-token / OAuth access-token verifier and dispatches four actions:
    - `status`: Reads current effective `Entitlement` (including `hasCustomerPortal` whenever `provider_customer_id` exists, even after `canceled`, so churned subscribers can still access historical VAT receipts).
    - `checkout`: Validates server-configured `STRIPE_MONTHLY_PRICE_ID` and constructs `success_url`, `cancel_url`, and `return_url` strictly from a server-configured `APP_PUBLIC_URL` allowlist (never trusting client body URLs or unvalidated `Origin` headers). Redirects to Customer Portal whenever `user_subscriptions.status IN ('active', 'trialing', 'past_due', 'unpaid', 'paused')` regardless of `cancel_at_period_end` (preventing overlapping subscriptions and orphan `past_due` invoices); when creating a new Checkout Session after `canceled`, reuses `customer = provider_customer_id`, sets `managed_payments[enabled] = true`, pins an explicit `Stripe-Version` header, and binds `client_reference_id = caller.userId` + `subscription_data[metadata][user_id] = caller.userId`.
    - `portal`: Mints a fresh authenticated Stripe Billing Customer Portal session (`POST /v1/billing_portal/sessions`) whenever `provider_customer_id` exists and returns `{ portalUrl }`.
    - `redeem-invite`: Enforces per-user/IP rate limiting against code enumeration, trims and uppercases the code, and executes an atomic redemption RPC (returning idempotent `200` without incrementing `used_count` if the caller re-submits their already-active code, rejecting self-re-redemption of a revoked grant, and allowing expired grants to be replaced by a new valid code).
  - **`billing-webhook` Edge Function (Transactional Idempotency, Concurrency Guard + Stripe Basil+ Schema Support):**
    - Verifies raw payload `Stripe-Signature` (`t` + `v1` HMAC with 300s replay window) using `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and pins explicit `Stripe-Version`.
    - Performs a read-only fast-path check on `processed_webhook_events(event_id)` (`200` if already committed) and extracts the subscription ID across both post-2025 Basil+ and legacy Stripe schemas (`invoice.parent.subscription_details.subscription ?? invoice.subscription` for invoice events).
    - If `subscription_id` exists in `pending_subscription_cancellations` (tombstoned from an offline-Stripe account deletion), immediately cancels it on Stripe (`DELETE /v1/subscriptions/{id}`), deletes the tombstone, and returns `200` without recreating local user state!
    - Otherwise fetches the canonical subscription via `GET /v1/subscriptions/{id}` (reading `items.data[0].current_period_end ?? current_period_end`), and commits `processed_webhook_events(event_id)` + `user_subscriptions` upsert inside **one atomic PL/pgSQL RPC** with a conditional monotonic write guard (`WHERE user_subscriptions.provider_subscription_id != EXCLUDED.provider_subscription_id OR (user_subscriptions.status != 'canceled' AND EXCLUDED.last_event_created >= user_subscriptions.last_event_created)`) so concurrent webhook isolates with different `event.id`s can never overwrite newer or terminal subscription states!
  - **`ai-coach` Edge Function:** Enforces the unified `Entitlement` + `Quota` + `O(1)` daily spend circuit-breaker gate prior to upstream Gemini execution (while allowing `action: "recover"` to deliver already-paid in-flight completions without re-checking `402`), derives granular `request_kind` (`initial_plan` when mode is planning with zero prior logs/summaries, `plan_regeneration` when mode is planning with existing training history, `plan_update` when plan modification is enabled in execution mode, `session_briefing` when auto-triggered without a user question, `chat` when a user question is present, and `classify` for exercise classification), includes `entitlement` (with `evaluatedAt`) in `200`/`402` responses, and registers async `TokenUsage` persistence via `waitUntil` on **both** `coach` and `classify` paths.
  - **`delete-account` Edge Function (GDPR-Immediate Erasure + Self-Healing Cancellation Tombstone):** Checks `user_subscriptions` first; if a Stripe subscription exists in any non-terminated state (`active`, `trialing`, `past_due`, `unpaid`, `paused`), attempts `DELETE /v1/subscriptions/{provider_subscription_id}` synchronously (`404` / `resource_missing` / already `canceled` treated as cancelled). Regardless of whether Stripe succeeds immediately or suffers a transient outage, executes one atomic cleanup RPC that **immediately** revokes `Entitlement` and erases all personal app rows (`user_subscriptions`, `invite_grants`, `ai_daily_usage`, `ai_request_results`), truly anonymizes `ai_monthly_token_usage` (`'deleted:' || gen_random_uuid()` per `(user_id, year_month)`), and—only if upstream Stripe cancellation failed—inserts the non-personal `provider_subscription_id` into `pending_subscription_cancellations` (returning `{ deleted: true, billingCancellationPending: true }` for transparent UI notice while `billing-webhook` / operator check cancels `provider_subscription_id` on next Stripe contact).

- **Granular Gemini `TokenUsage` Metering, Stream-Abort Handling & SQL Cost Views:**
  - Extend the shared Gemini client to extract and accumulate per-attempt `usageMetadata` (`promptTokenCount`, `cachedContentTokenCount`, `candidatesTokenCount`, `thoughtsTokenCount`) **before** evaluating `finishReason !== "STOP"` or Zod response parsing so exact billed tokens are captured even when an attempt fails validation or `MAX_TOKENS` and triggers model fallback!
  - Only when an upstream stream aborts at the network/transport layer before emitting `usageMetadata`, increment `unmeasured_count` and estimate fallback tokens conservatively (`ceil(chars / 4)`).
  - Cover all configured primary and fallback coaching/classification models in the server pricing table with a conservative non-zero default fallback rate for future model IDs, updating both `ai_monthly_token_usage` and `ai_daily_global_usage.estimated_cost_usd_micros` atomically.

## Testing Decisions

- **Philosophy of Good Tests:** Tests must assert observable external behavior, HTTP status codes, returned payloads, persisted state transitions, and user-visible view-model outputs at public module and handler boundaries—never internal helper calls or private store refs. External network boundaries (Stripe REST API, Google JWKS, Gemini HTTP stream, Supabase RPC client) are injected via handler dependency ports or module gateway fakes.
- **Seams & Modules Under Test:**
  1. **Backend `ai-coach` & Shared Gemini Seam:** Extend the existing `ai-coach` request handler tests and shared Gemini adapter tests to verify `Entitlement` precedence (`Subscription` > `InviteGrant` > `FreeAllowance` > `402 payment-required`), 5/day free-phase cap vs. 15/day paid cap, global daily cost circuit-breaker (`AI_DAILY_COST_CAP_USD`), idempotent `FreeAllowance` refund on upstream failure **without** reversing recorded `TokenUsage` costs, granular `request_kind` classification, and `usageMetadata` + aborted-stream fallback cost capture across primary and fallback models.
  2. **Backend `billing` & `billing-webhook` Seams:** Test `handleBillingRequest` (`status`, `checkout` server-enforced price ID + Managed Payments flags + subject metadata binding + duplicate-subscription guard, `portal` fresh session lookup, `redeem-invite` validation/exhaustion/expiry) and `handleBillingWebhook` (`Stripe-Signature` raw-body HMAC + replay-window verification, `processed_webhook_events` idempotency on duplicate `event.id`, concurrent out-of-order webhook updates with distinct `event.id`s, separate handling of invoice vs. subscription events via canonical `GET /v1/subscriptions/{id}` lookup, self-healing cancellation of tombstoned `pending_subscription_cancellations`, rejection of missing `user_id` metadata, 3-day `past_due` grace preservation, recovery, and period-end cancellation). Include declarative SQL schema contract tests alongside existing quota schema tests.
  3. **Backend `delete-account` Seam:** Extend existing `delete-account` handler tests to verify synchronous upstream Stripe subscription cancellation when reachable, immediate GDPR row erasure + `pending_subscription_cancellations` tombstone creation (`billingCancellationPending: true`) when Stripe is unreachable, idempotent handling of users without active subscriptions, retention of versioned pseudonymous `subject_digest` `FreeAllowance` counters (and 24-month inactivity pruning rule), and true random-UUID anonymization (`deleted:<uuid>`) of `ai_monthly_token_usage` rows.
  4. **Client `billing` & `aiCoach` Presentation Seams + Architecture Boundaries:** Test `useBillingStore` through its public presentation facade (offline cache hydration, `?invite=CODE` redemption, `?checkout=success` server-verified polling, `Entitlement` state transitions), extend `useAiOrchestratorStore` and `CoachTab` / `PlanTab` tests to verify unthrottled auto-briefings during `FreeAllowance` and silent pause + Upgrade Card ("With Pro" vs. "Always Free") rendering when exhausted, and verify zero layer violations via the existing architecture boundary test suite.
- **Prior Art in Codebase:** Follow the dependency-injected Deno/Vitest handler test style established in existing `ai-coach`, `auth-token`, `delete-account`, and `quotaSchema` tests, and the Pinia + view-model seam test style in `aiOrchestratorStore` and `CoachTab` tests.

## Out of Scope

- **Annual Subscription Package:** Only the single monthly `7.99 EUR/month` plan is offered at launch (though `price_id` storage supports adding an annual price later without schema redesign).
- **Native iOS App Store / Google Play In-App Purchases (RevenueCat / StoreKit / Play Billing):** Deferred until native mobile store distribution; choosing Stripe now plus decoupling `Entitlement` ensures seamless RevenueCat unification later.
- **Custom Admin Dashboard UI:** Managing `invite_codes` and inspecting AI unit economics (`v_ai_unit_economics_monthly`, `v_ai_user_costs`) are performed directly in Supabase Studio at launch.
- **Historical Multi-Version SQL Pricing Tables, 2-Phase Pre-Call Spend Reservations & Background Cron Workers:** Excluded intentionally as unnecessary complexity—token costs are materialized in USD micro-units at write time, per-call token exposure is bounded upfront via `maxOutputTokens` and 2-attempt caps (avoiding leaked reservations on isolate crashes), and offline-Stripe account deletions self-heal via a non-personal `pending_subscription_cancellations` tombstone without background worker queues.
- **Embedded In-App Checkout Scripts (`Stripe.js`) & Custom Checkout Domains:** Excluded intentionally in favor of server-generated Hosted Checkout redirects (Managed Payments uses Stripe/Link hosted checkout domains and Link/Customer Portal management) to preserve strict Content-Security-Policy isolation and zero third-party client bundle bloat.
- **Gating Non-AI Features:** Manual workout logging, historical workout access, Google Sheets sync, and deterministic sports-science calculations remain permanently un-gated.
